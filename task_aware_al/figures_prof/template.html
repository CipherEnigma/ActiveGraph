BTP progress review

Choosing which links to check, so a chosen set of nodes gets classified correctly

Part of a network is hidden and each check costs something. This page shows what has been built, what the experiments found, one result that did not transfer to real data, and where the project goes next.

The setup

Each query asks one yes/no question, and the answer feeds the classifier

Hidden network, 15% of links visible, a few labelled nodes, 15 target nodes whose labels we want. A budget of B queries. Success is accuracy on the targets after the budget is spent. Strategies differ only in which pair they pick next.

Finding 1 · synthetic graphs, 30 seeds

Finding more real links did not improve classification

After 200 queries at 15% labelled nodes. Left: real links found. Right: accuracy gain on the target nodes. Common neighbours finds the most links (9.9) and gains 0.031. Value on targets finds fewer (7.9) and gains 0.227. A link can be real and still useless, for example when it joins nodes no label can reach.

Finding 2 · accuracy against budget

The value strategy keeps improving past 200 queries, with falling returns

Mean accuracy gain on the 15 targets (original seeds 0–29). Gain added per extra 100 queries at 15% labels: +0.13, +0.09, +0.05, +0.02. No strategy closes 75% of the gap to full-graph accuracy within 400 queries.

Finding 3 · what drives the gain

Aiming at the targets gives most of the gain; asking one at a time adds a smaller one

Accuracy gain at 200 queries, 15% labels. Targeting the objective at the 15 targets instead of all unlabelled nodes adds about +0.12. Choosing adaptively instead of all at once adds about +0.05. The decomposition has one caveat: the target version also restricts its shortlist to pairs that can affect the targets, and that effect is not yet separated from the objective.

Verification

Checks run so far

Fresh seeds. Scorer tuned on seeds 0–2. Repeated on unseen seeds 100–129; results within about 0.03.

No label leakage. Randomising every non-training label changed none of the queries any strategy chose.

Three ways of hiding links. Random, targets-hidden, hub-biased. Value closes 0.46–0.59 of the gap, best heuristic at most 0.13.

Exact reproduction. Re-running earlier experiments reproduces them with difference 0. Paired t-test, Wilcoxon, bootstrap CI and Holm correction used.

200 queries, 5 labelled papers per class, 50 targets, 15% of links visible. No difference is significant with 3 seeds. A longer test (1,000 queries, one seed) and five further seeds at 200 queries were inconsistent: value led on two seeds, tied on two and trailed on one.

Left: at 15% visibility, 1,642 of Cora's 2,708 nodes have no visible link at all. Right: 200 queries add about 28% to the visible links in the synthetic setup but only about 5% in Cora, so there is little for any classifier to use. The classifier based on node text (GCN) also already gets most of the accuracy from features, which leaves little room.

Other findings from this pilot. The pool of candidate pairs recovers only 33% of hidden links, and the link-probability model is weak (AUC about 0.63). A 400-node Cora subgraph left the GCN already at its ceiling, so it was not a useful test either.

Where this is going

The claim is fixed in advance, and the next step is a fair real-data test

Done

Synthetic benchmark and the main comparison

Eight strategies, static and adaptive, 30 original and 30 fresh seeds, leakage and reproduction checks.

Done

Cora pipeline

Sparse label-propagation scorer, candidate pool, link-probability model, GCN evaluator that is separate from the scorer, pilot run.

Done

Success criterion written down

Value must beat common neighbours, max-degree and link-probability-only on the area under the accuracy-versus-budget curve, on 20 or more fresh seeds, paired tests with correction. The file also lists what result would make us drop or narrow the claim.

Next

A Cora setting where the budget is meaningful

Higher link visibility and a budget in the thousands, or a graph where features alone are weak. Settings are fixed before the confirmatory run.

Next

Ablation and upper bound

Separate the target objective from the shortlist restriction, and add an oracle strategy that shows how much room remains.

Later

Stronger competitors and a better link model

Embedding-based link probabilities in the style of ALPINE, query by committee, and a second real dataset.

If value does not beat the best baseline on the fresh real-data seeds, the claim narrows to synthetic community graphs, and the report says so.
